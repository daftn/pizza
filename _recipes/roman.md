*From the Roman-style Pizza Dough in the Gozney cookbook. High hydration and a good dose of olive oil make it light, crisp and golden.*

*Notes on the source:*
- *The book's batch (1,000g of flour) adds up to about 1,780g, about 160g more than the 9 × 180g it says it makes. This version is scaled down to match, with the same percentages and a small buffer for dough lost to the bowl.*
- *The book lists bread flour as 30% and semolina as 70%, but the grams say the opposite: bread flour is 70% and semolina 30%. The grams are what's used here.*
- *The book lists olive oil but never says when to add it. It goes in right after the salt (a handwritten note in our copy).*

## Mix

Done in a stand or spiral mixer on low and medium speeds.

1. **Dissolve the yeast.** Put the [[water]] first water and [[yeast]] yeast in the bowl and run on low for a couple of minutes.
2. **Add the flours.** Add the [[flour]] bread flour and [[semolina]] semolina and mix on low for 5 minutes, until there are no dry bits.
3. **Rest.** Stop the mixer for 1–2 minutes.
4. **Second water, slowly.** Back on low, add the [[water2]] second water a little at a time, letting each splash disappear before the next. This takes about 5 minutes. Keep the last 10–20g back.
5. **Salt.** Add the [[salt]] salt with that last splash of water and mix on medium for 2 minutes until it's fully combined.
6. **Olive oil.** With the mixer on medium, drizzle in the [[oil]] olive oil slowly until it's absorbed.
7. **Rest 5 minutes**, then turn the dough out.
   - *Freezing:* to freeze any of it, divide it into portions now and freeze them before the dough starts fermenting.

## Ferment

1. **Bulk (1–2 hours).** Form one big ball, cover it in a bowl and leave it at room temperature.
2. **Ball.** Divide into [[count]] pieces of [[weight]], shape them into balls and put them in a lidded dough tray.
3. **Room temperature (1 hour)**, then into the fridge.
4. **Cold ferment (24–48 hours).**
5. **Warm up (3+ hours).** Take the balls out at least 3 hours before baking. Warmer rooms need less time.
   - *Same-day option:* skip the fridge and proof the balls at room temperature for 6–8 hours.
