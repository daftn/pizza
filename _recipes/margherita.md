## Sauce

1. **Crush by hand.** Tip the [[tomatoes]] tomatoes into a bowl and squeeze them through your fingers. Leave it chunky; don't blend.
2. **Season.** Mix in the [[sauce_salt]] salt, [[sauce_oil]] olive oil and a few torn basil leaves. Taste and add salt if it needs it.
   - If the can is very watery, pour off some of the thin juice first, or the center of the pizza will go soggy.

## Prep the cheese

Fresh mozzarella holds a lot of water. Tear it into chunks about **an hour ahead** and let it drain in a strainer (or on paper towels) in the fridge. Pat dry before using.

## Build

1. **Stretch** the dough ball to about 12 inches, keeping a puffy rim.
2. **Top in this order:** spread [[sauce]] sauce, leaving the rim bare. Add a few basil leaves, sprinkle [[pecorino]] pecorino, drizzle olive oil, then scatter [[mozz]] mozzarella. Keep it light in the middle.

## Bake: outdoor oven

1. **Preheat to 800–900°F on the stone**, checked with an infrared thermometer.
2. **Bake 60–90 seconds**, turning the pizza every 15–20 seconds so the side by the flame doesn't burn.
3. **Done** when the rim is puffed with leopard spots and the cheese has melted.

## Finish

Top with a few fresh basil leaves and a little more grated pecorino.
