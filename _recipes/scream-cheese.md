## Prep

1. **Sauce.** Use the sauce from [Mozzferatu](../charlies-ny-cheese/).
2. **Cream cheese.** Take it out of the fridge about an hour ahead so it's soft enough to dollop.

## Build

1. **Stretch** the dough ball to about 14 inches.
2. **Top in this order:**
   1. Sauce, spread thin, leaving the rim bare.
   2. Mozzarella.
   3. Pepperoni.
   4. Cream cheese in small dollops spread around the pizza.

## Bake: outdoor oven

1. **Preheat to about 700°F on the stone**, checked with an infrared thermometer.
2. **Bake 2–3 minutes**, turning the pizza every 20–30 seconds.
3. **Done** when the rim is browned, the pepperoni edges have curled and the cream cheese has softened and started to color.
