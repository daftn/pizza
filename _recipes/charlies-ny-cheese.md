*From [Charlie Anderson's Authentic New York-Style Pizza](https://charlieandersoncooking.com/recipes/authentic-new-york-style-pizza).*

## Preheat

1. Put a **baking steel on the second-highest rack** (a stone goes on the lowest rack) and preheat to **550°F (287°C) for about 90 minutes**. Take the dough out of the fridge when you start.

## Build

1. Stretch a dough ball and lay it on a wooden peel dusted with semola.
2. Top in this order: [[sauce]] sauce, a pinch of oregano if you like, [[pecorino]] pecorino, then [[mozz]] mozzarella. Go light on sauce and cheese in the center so it doesn't get soggy.

## Bake

1. Bake **4–5 minutes**, until the cheese is fully melted and just starting to separate. Rotate halfway if you want.
   - If the bottom is burning before the top is done, slide a pizza screen underneath for the last 1–2 minutes.
2. Leave the oven on. Cool the pizza on a wire rack for **at least 15 minutes**, then slice.
3. **Reheat** each slice on the steel at 550°F for **60–90 seconds**, until the bottom is as charred as you like. That's the NY slice-shop finish.
