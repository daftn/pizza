*From the Signature-style Pizza Dough in the Gozney cookbook. Their go-to starter dough, built to be forgiving.*

*Note on the source: the listed amounts add up to about 1,650g, more than either yield it gives (4 × 350g or 6 × 250g), so expect some extra dough. A couple of the ounce conversions in the book are also off; the grams are what's used here.*

## Mix

Done in a stand or spiral mixer on low and medium speeds. Stop now and then to scrape the bowl so no flour hides at the bottom.

1. **Dissolve the yeast.** Put the [[water]] first water and [[yeast]] yeast in the bowl and run on low for about 2 minutes.
2. **Add the flour.** Add the [[flour]] flour and mix on low for 5 minutes, until there are no dry patches.
3. **Rest.** Stop the mixer for 1–2 minutes.
4. **Salt.** Add the [[salt]] salt with the [[water2]] second water and mix on medium for 2 minutes until the salt disappears.
5. **Oil.** Rest a few minutes, then run on medium and pour in the [[oil]] olive oil slowly until it's absorbed.
6. **Rest again** for 5 minutes, then turn the dough out.

## Ferment

1. **Ball.** Divide into [[count]] pieces of [[weight]], shape into balls and put them in a lidded dough tray.
2. **Room temperature (1 hour)**, then into the fridge.
3. **Cold ferment (24–48 hours).**
4. **Warm up (3–6 hours).** Take the balls out and let them come to room temperature before baking. Warmer rooms need less time.
   - *Same-day option:* skip the fridge and proof the balls at room temperature for 8–10 hours.
