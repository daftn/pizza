*From Leo Spizzirri's [Authentic Neapolitan Pizza (VPN-Approved)](https://www.adamwitt.co/allrecipes/z68a5tgnm8njf6j-hfxcw-gz2p4-b3geg-k3bmn-36ywb-hnn2w-cmjp7-msb9w-jega5-wyfy4-sgmdf-4ggt2-n2ts6-bskm7-r72wd-6xter-bfj5l). The original uses 1g of fresh yeast; this page swaps in the equivalent instant yeast.*

*Notes on the source:*
- *The yeast is very low on purpose: 0.06% fresh yeast, which is about 0.02% instant. That's what lets the dough sit out for up to a day without a fridge.*
- *It says it makes 10–15 pizzas of 10–14 inches, but the only ball size it gives is 180g for a 10-inch. The whole batch makes 15 of those; use the size buttons for bigger pies.*

**Measuring the yeast:** 0.35g is too small for most kitchen scales. Either use a 0.01g jeweler's scale, or dissolve **1g of instant yeast in 30g of water** and use **10g of that liquid** (about 0.33g of yeast). If you use the liquid, take that 10g out of the recipe's water.

## Mix (by hand)

1. **Salt water.** Stir the [[salt]] sea salt into the [[water]] cold water in a large bowl until it dissolves.
2. **Start the batter.** Add roughly a tenth of the [[flour]] flour and mix it in with your hand until it looks like pancake batter. Add the [[yeast]] yeast and mix until it disappears.
3. **Add the rest of the flour** about a tenth at a time, mixing each addition in before adding the next. A few small beads of flour are fine.
4. **Rest 20 minutes**, covered.
5. **Knead** on a clean, unfloured counter until smooth, then **rest 15 minutes**, covered.

## Ferment (no fridge)

1. **Portion.** Pull off a log-shaped piece of the dough at a time and weigh out [[count]] pieces of [[weight]].
2. **Ball.** Shape each into a tight ball, set them in a dough tray and cover.
3. **Room temperature, 8–24 hours.** A warm room is fast, a cool room is slow. With this little yeast, plan on the longer end in a normal room. They're ready when they've roughly doubled and feel light and airy.
