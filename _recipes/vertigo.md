> *Don't look down.* A bone-white canvas of melted mozzarella and Muenster, trapped in a dizzying double spiral of neon-orange vodka sauce and emerald basil pesto, studded with crispy soppressata, a snowfall of pecorino and parmigiano, and a final strike of hot honey.

The first Fear Fest pie, for the Alfred Hitchcock year. Also known as *Spiral Into Madness* or *The Bell Tower*.

You'll need **two squeeze bottles** with the tips snipped to about a ¼-inch opening.

## Sauce prep (1–2 hours ahead)

1. **Reduce the vodka sauce.** Simmer the [[vodka]] vodka sauce with the [[chili]] red pepper flakes in a small pan over medium heat for 5–7 minutes, stirring now and then, until a spoon dragged through it leaves a brief trail. Take it a little thicker than seems right: the cream thins it back out, and a thick sauce holds a sharper line.
2. **Add the glow.** Off the heat, stir in the [[cream]] heavy cream. It turns from brick red to an opaque, bright pastel orange. Let it cool to room temperature, then fill bottle #1.
3. **Loosen the pesto.** Whisk the [[pesto_oil]] olive oil into the room-temperature [[pesto]] pesto until glossy and smooth, then fill bottle #2.
4. **Keep both bottles on the counter.** Never pipe ice-cold sauce onto a hot pie.

## Build: cheese first

1. **Stretch** a 380–420g dough ball to 14 inches with a defined rim, and move it to a peel dusted with semolina. (In the dough calculator, set grams each to about 400.)
2. **Cheese.** Scatter the [[mozz]] mozzarella and [[muenster]] Muenster evenly, stopping about ½ inch short of the rim. Cheese on the crust edge burns and flattens the rim. Going cheese-first keeps moisture off the dough and makes a flat white canvas for the spiral.
3. **Soppressata.** Stack 5–6 rounds of the [[sopp]] soppressata and cut them into strips about ½ inch wide. The curved strips from the edges follow the spiral nicely. Lay them end to end, overlapping a little, in a spiral from the center out, leaving **2–2½ inches** between the rings (room for the sauce line plus the pesto track).
4. **Pipe the orange spiral.** Starting in the dead center, squeeze one steady, continuous ribbon of vodka sauce right **alongside** the soppressata, touching its inner edge but on the cheese, not on the meat. The sauce caramelizes into the cheese, and the meat stays exposed so it crisps.
   - That leaves about **1–1½ inches of white cheese** between each orange line and the next ring of soppressata: that's the track for the green spiral.

## Bake: outdoor oven

Use [New York (Outdoor Oven)](../charlies-ny-outdoor/) or [The Default](../everyday-dough/).

1. **Preheat the stone to 700–750°F** on a medium-high flame.
2. **Launch, then drop the flame to medium-low** so the cheese melts evenly and the vodka sauce caramelizes without scorching.
3. **Turn every 30–45 seconds** for a 3–4 minute bake, until the rim is blistered and the soppressata edges are crisp and sizzling.
4. **Rest 60 seconds** on a cooling rack so the cheese sets a little.

## The reveal

Do this at the table.

1. **Slice first.** Cut 8 slices with a rocker blade or wheel and keep them pushed together in a circle. Slicing after the pesto drags green across the white and orange.
2. **Pipe the green spiral** into the white tracks between the orange rings, from the center out.
3. **The final strike.** Zig-zag or spiral the [[honey]] hot honey over the whole pie, then finish with a heavy snowfall of the [[hard_cheese]] grated pecorino and parmigiano around the rim. Serve right away.
