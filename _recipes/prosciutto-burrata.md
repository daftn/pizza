## Prep

1. **Fresh mozzarella.** It holds a lot of water. Tear it into chunks about **an hour ahead** and let it drain in a strainer (or on paper towels) in the fridge. Pat dry before using.
2. **Burrata.** Take it out of the fridge about 30 minutes ahead so it isn't ice-cold on the hot pizza.

## Build

1. **Stretch** the dough ball to about 12 inches, keeping a puffy rim.
2. **Top:** drizzle the base with olive oil. No sauce on this one; the tomatoes do that job.
   1. Smoked mozzarella (or smoked provolone).
   2. Fresh mozzarella.
   3. Tomatoes, cut side up, with a pinch of salt.

## Bake: outdoor oven

1. **Preheat to 800–900°F on the stone**, checked with an infrared thermometer.
2. **Bake 60–90 seconds**, turning the pizza every 15–20 seconds so the side by the flame doesn't burn.
3. **Done** when the rim is puffed with leopard spots, the cheese has melted and the tomatoes have just started to blister.

## Bake: home oven

A home oven can't get close to outdoor-oven heat, so the crust will be paler and a bit crisper. It's still a good pizza.

1. **Preheat at the oven's max (500–550°F) for 45–60 minutes** with a baking steel or stone on the top rack, about 6 inches below the broiler.
2. **Bake 6–8 minutes**, until the bottom is browned.
3. **Switch to broil for the last 1–2 minutes** to color the rim and blister the tomatoes. Watch it closely; it goes fast.

## Finish

Work quickly so the pizza is still hot.

1. **Prosciutto first.** Drape it over the hot pizza so it softens without cooking.
2. **Arugula** on top of the prosciutto.
3. **Burrata in the middle.** Set it in the center and tear it open so the creamy inside spills out.
4. **Drizzle** with olive oil, then add black pepper and flaky salt.
