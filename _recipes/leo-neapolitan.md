*From Leo Spizzirri's Neapolitan dough: classic Naples style, fermented overnight at room temperature and never refrigerated.*

*Notes on the source:*
- *The ingredients say instant dry yeast, but the method says to press fresh yeast between your fingers. This page uses the 3g of instant yeast; just sprinkle it in. (Fresh yeast would be about 3× the weight, roughly 9g.)*
- *The source doesn't give a ball size. 6 × 270g uses the whole batch and is a classic 12-inch Neapolitan ball.*

## Mix (by hand)

1. **Salt water.** Stir the [[salt]] sea salt into the [[water]] cold water in a large bowl until it dissolves.
2. **Start the batter.** Add roughly a tenth of the [[flour]] flour and mix it in with your hand until it looks like pancake batter. Sprinkle in the [[yeast]] yeast and mix until it disappears.
3. **Add the rest of the flour** a handful at a time, about a tenth at a time, mixing each one in before adding the next. A few small beads of flour are fine.
4. **Rest 20 minutes**, covered.
5. **Knead** on a clean, unfloured counter until smooth, then **rest 15 minutes**, covered.

## Ferment (no fridge)

1. **Ball.** Divide into [[count]] pieces of [[weight]], shape them into tight balls and put them in a dough tray. Cover.
2. **Room temperature, 8–24 hours.** A warm room is fast, a cool room is slow. They're ready when they've roughly doubled and feel light and airy.
