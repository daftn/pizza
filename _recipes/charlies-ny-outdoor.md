*From Charlie Anderson's [How to Make REAL NY-Style Pizza in an Outdoor Pizza Oven](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pzj8tjhq-2o). For a home oven, use [New York (Home Oven)](../charlies-ny/).*

## Mix

1. **First mix (low, ~4 minutes).** Add the [[water]] water to the mixer bowl, then the [[flour]] flour and [[yeast]] yeast. Mix on low just until the dough comes together. Mixing by hand works too.
2. **Rest (20 minutes).** Cover the bowl and let the gluten develop on its own. Optional, but it noticeably improves the texture.
3. **Salt (medium, ~3 minutes).** Add the [[salt]] salt and mix on medium until the dough *just* starts to turn smooth. Don't overmix: this is a strong flour, and too much gluten development makes it hard to stretch.

## Ferment

1. **Short bulk (1–2 hours).** Move the dough to a clear container so you can watch it. You only want it to rise **about 10%**, not double.
2. **Ball.** Divide into [[count]] pieces of [[weight]] and ball them up.
3. **Cold ferment (2+ days, 3–4 ideal).** Put each ball in a dough tin or any short, wide container (narrow ones don't leave room to spread) and refrigerate.

## Bake day

1. **Pull the dough 3+ hours ahead**, up to 5–6 hours if your fridge runs cold. If a ball still feels tight, press it down a little to make stretching easier later.
2. **It's ready when it has roughly doubled** since going into the tin. Under-proofed dough is hard to stretch and won't rise as well in the oven.
