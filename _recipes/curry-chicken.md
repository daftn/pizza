## Prep

1. **Chicken.** Use any roasted chicken: leftover or rotisserie. Dice or shred it and toss it with a spoonful of the curry sauce so it doesn't dry out in the oven.
2. **Tomatoes.** Slice them, scoop out the seeds and pat the slices dry, or their juice pools in the middle of the pizza.
3. **Pepper and onion.** Slice both thin. The pepper stays crisp through the short bake.

## Build

1. **Stretch** the dough ball to about 14 inches.
2. **Top in this order:**
   1. Curry sauce, spread thin, leaving the rim bare. A thick sauce works best; a thin one makes the middle soggy.
   2. Mozzarella.
   3. Chicken.
   4. Yellow pepper, tomato and red onion.
   5. *Optional:* mascarpone in small dollops spread around the pizza. It melts into creamy pools that go well with the curry.

## Bake: home oven

Use [New York (Home Oven)](../charlies-ny/) or [The All-Rounder](../everyday-dough/).

1. **Preheat at the oven's max (500–550°F) for 45–60 minutes** with a baking steel or stone on the top rack.
2. **Bake 7–9 minutes**, until the bottom is browned and the cheese is bubbling.

## Bake: outdoor oven

Use [New York (Outdoor Oven)](../charlies-ny-outdoor/) or [The All-Rounder](../everyday-dough/).

1. **Preheat to about 650°F on the stone**, checked with an infrared thermometer.
2. **Bake 2–3 minutes**, turning the pizza every 20–30 seconds.
3. **Done** when the rim is browned and the cheese is bubbling.

## Finish

Lay thin lime slices over the top, then scatter torn basil and chopped cilantro. Squeeze a slice over a piece before eating if you want more lime.
