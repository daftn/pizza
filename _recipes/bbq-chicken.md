## Prep

1. **Chicken.** Use any cooked chicken: leftover, rotisserie or grilled. Dice it small and toss it with a spoonful of BBQ sauce so it doesn't dry out in the oven.
2. **Bacon.** Cook it until it's *almost* crisp, then chop it. It finishes crisping on the pizza.
3. **Onion.** Slice it as thin as you can so it softens in the bake.

## Build

1. **Stretch** the dough ball to about 14 inches.
2. **Top in this order:**
   1. [[sauce]] BBQ sauce, spread thin, leaving the rim bare.
   2. [[gouda]] smoked gouda, **straight on the sauce**.
   3. [[chicken]] chicken and [[onion]] red onion.
   4. [[group:Four-cheese blend]] four-cheese blend over everything, which keeps the chicken from drying out.
   5. [[bacon]] bacon on top so it crisps.

## Bake: home oven

Use [New York (Home Oven)](../charlies-ny/) or [The All-Rounder](../everyday-dough/).

1. **Preheat at the oven's max (500–550°F) for 45–60 minutes** with a baking steel or stone on the top rack.
2. **Bake 7–9 minutes**, until the cheese is bubbling and the bottom is browned.

## Bake: outdoor oven

Use [New York (Outdoor Oven)](../charlies-ny-outdoor/) or [The All-Rounder](../everyday-dough/).

1. **Preheat to about 600–650°F on the stone**, checked with an infrared thermometer. Keep it on the cooler side: BBQ sauce has a lot of sugar and scorches fast.
2. **Bake 2–3 minutes**, turning the pizza every 20–30 seconds.
3. **Done** when the cheese is bubbling and the rim is browned. If the sauce at the edges is going black, turn the flame down.

## Finish

Scatter a pinch of chopped cilantro over the top and slice.
